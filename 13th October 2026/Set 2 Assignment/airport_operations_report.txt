Flight operations were delayed during the early morning because of dense fog near the eastern runway.

Ground staff reported reduced visibility and several aircraft were kept at their parking bays.

The weather improved gradually after 9 AM and departures resumed in phases.

One aircraft required an additional technical inspection before departure.

Passengers on delayed flights were informed through the airport notification system.

The northern runway remained fully operational throughout the incident.

No emergency situations were reported.

Airport management requested a detailed review of the delays and communication process.

A follow-up meeting has been scheduled with the operations, weather, and ground handling teams.
