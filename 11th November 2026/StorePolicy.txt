RETAIL SUPPORT STORE — INTERNAL POLICY GUIDE (FICTIONAL)

Shipping
- Standard delivery: 3–5 business days.
- Express delivery: 1–2 business days.
- International shipping is not available.
- Shipping charges are displayed at checkout; the agent must not invent an amount.

Returns
- Returns are accepted within 7 calendar days of delivery if items meet the return conditions.
- Damaged items must be reported within 48 hours of delivery.
- Returned electronics must include the original packaging and accessories.
- Gift cards are not returnable.

Refunds
- Approved returns are refunded within 5 business days after approval.
- Refunds are sent back to the original payment method.
- The assistant cannot initiate or confirm a refund without an order system connection.

Warranty
- Electronics include a 12-month limited warranty against manufacturing defects.
- Accidental damage is not covered by the standard warranty.

Order status
- Live order tracking and order changes are not connected to the assistant.
- Never invent a tracking number, delivery date, refund status or order information.

Guidelines
- Answer using this document when relevant.
- If a policy is not listed, state that it is not available and refer the customer to human support.
