HARBORLIGHT SCIENCE MUSEUM - WORKSHOPS AND EVENTS
Training Edition 1.0 | Fictional workshop schedule

WORKSHOP OVERVIEW
Harborlight Science Museum runs beginner-friendly science workshops. Workshop seats must be booked separately from general museum admission. All fees below are in Indian rupees. The following programs and policies are fictional teaching data.

ROBOTICS STARTER LAB
The Robotics Starter Lab takes place every Saturday from 11:00 AM to 12:30 PM. It is designed for participants aged 12 and above. The workshop costs INR 600 per participant and has a maximum of 20 seats. Basic robotics kits and laptops are provided. Participants build a simple sensor-based prototype and take home their worksheet, not the robotics kit.

SPACE EXPLORERS SESSION
Space Explorers is held every Sunday from 2:00 PM to 3:00 PM. It is suitable for children aged 9 and above. The session costs INR 400 per participant and has 24 seats. Participants learn about planets and complete a paper mission-planning activity. No laptop is required.

CHEMISTRY DISCOVERY WORKSHOP
Chemistry Discovery runs on Fridays from 1:00 PM to 2:15 PM. It is for participants aged 13 and above and costs INR 500 per participant. Safety goggles are supplied by the museum and must be worn during experiments. Food, drinks, and open footwear are not permitted in the chemistry room.

BOOKING RULES
Workshop reservations should be completed at least two days before the session. A parent or guardian must accompany participants younger than 12 years to the registration desk. A workshop ticket does not include a museum admission ticket. Workshop places are assigned in booking order until the session is full.

CANCELLATIONS
A participant can request a workshop credit if they cancel at least 48 hours before the workshop. Requests made later than that are not eligible for credit. If the museum cancels a workshop, participants receive a full refund for the workshop fee.

ADDITIONAL INFORMATION
Workshop instructors provide the necessary learning materials. Participants should arrive 15 minutes early. The museum does not provide transport to workshops and this sample schedule does not mention an online attendance option.
