GREENTRAIL BIKE RENTALS — CUSTOMER GUIDE
Training document (fictional company and policies)

1. OPERATING HOURS
GreenTrail Bike Rentals is open Monday through Saturday from 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM. On Sundays, the rental counter is open from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The last new rental starts one hour before closing.

2. RENTAL PRICES
A standard bicycle costs INR 250 for two hours or INR 600 for a full day. An electric bicycle costs INR 450 for two hours or INR 1,100 for a full day. All rentals require a refundable security deposit of INR 1,000 per bicycle.

3. BOOKING AND PAYMENT
Customers can reserve bicycles online or at the rental counter. Online reservations must be made at least two hours before pickup. Payment can be made by UPI, debit card, or credit card. Customers must show a valid photo ID when collecting a bicycle.

4. HELMETS AND SAFETY
A helmet is included at no additional charge with every rental. Riders must wear helmets throughout the ride. Children below 14 years must be accompanied by an adult. Customers should check the brakes and tires before leaving the rental counter.

5. CANCELLATION POLICY
Customers receive a full refund when they cancel at least 24 hours before their pickup time. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before pickup receive a 50% refund. No-shows are not eligible for a refund.

6. LATE RETURN POLICY
Bicycles returned more than 15 minutes after the scheduled return time incur a late fee of INR 100 for each additional started hour. Customers should contact the rental counter if they expect a delay.

7. BREAKDOWNS AND SUPPORT
In case of a mechanical breakdown, customers should stop riding and call the support desk at the number provided on their rental receipt. GreenTrail will arrange assistance during operating hours. Customers should not attempt major repairs themselves.

8. INFORMATION NOT PROVIDED
This guide does not specify delivery services, monthly memberships, or airport pickup. If customers ask about these topics, staff should explain that the information is not available in this guide.
